About Munition Tech

Decades of Excellence

A group of technologists and professionals with several decades of experience in the production of weapon systems and defence services joined together to provide technological support and knowledge services.

Our Expertise

We specialize in weapon systems design/development, production/industry support, quality assurance/inspection, and life cycle support services. Our areas of service include viability studies, market research, prototype development, assistance in planning production infrastructure and machinery.

MTPL assists Indian defence firms in joint ventures with foreign collaborators and in the training of their R&D personnel, contributing to Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives.
